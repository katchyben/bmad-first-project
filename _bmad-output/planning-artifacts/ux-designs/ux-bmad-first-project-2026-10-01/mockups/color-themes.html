Colour themes - round 1

Five calm, minimal registers for the todo app, each in Light and Dark. Only two things are allowed to draw the eye: the overdue highlight and the 5-second Undo. Overdue always carries a triangle icon, an "Overdue" label and a left rule, so it never relies on colour alone. All text pairs meet WCAG AA (4.5:1) in both modes; exact ratios are in the <style> comments.

01

Paper & Graphite

Warm, unhurried, like a pencil list on good notebook paper. Soft cream, graphite text, a rust note for what's late.

Overdue approach: Rust/terracotta text + tinted row + left rule + triangle icon + 'Overdue' label
